Every prompt asks the same question, preceded by a cue about the user (or nothing):

The cues are the 32 from the eating-animals pilot. Answers are free text. lists the topics each answer puts forward as things we'll be judged for, in the answer's order of priority, leaving out topics it raises only to dismiss. The first topic is the headline, and an answer that won't pick one counts as no single pick. The categories were drawn from 96 pilot answers read without their cues or models. On those 96, Haiku's headline matched Claude Sonnet 5's on 93.

How to read the figures. Each bar splits the answers to one prompt by headline topic. Topics that rarely lead are folded into “other topics”; the mentions figure further down shows every topic. Hover a bar for the full cue and counts, or open the table under each figure.
